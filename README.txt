Pizza Kon Khmer demo

Open index.html in a browser with internet access for Bootstrap and images.
Register through acc.html, then click your name in the navbar to open page.html.
Checkout requires login. Orders are stored locally in your browser.

Important: This is a FRONTEND CLASSROOM DEMO. Passwords are stored in localStorage and are NOT secure. Do not use real passwords. No real payment, backend, email delivery, or server-side authentication is implemented.
